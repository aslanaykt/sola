SOLA · Floating solar · Interaction layer

Unused water, read from the shore.

Solar farms take up land. SOLA puts them on water nobody uses: lakes, reservoirs, quiet rivers, the sea. Each 8-module unit joins the next to grow a field, a walkway deck gives access for service, and one monitoring system follows every unit, from the shore and from a tablet on board.

01 · Platform

A live twin of the object, a light on the water

On a laptop the platform appears as itself: the same pipes, brackets and floats, with every module's output floating above it and the one that needs attention opening its own card. On the water, the curled edge of each bracket carries a light strip that stays dark until one module differs from the others.

02 · Shore unit

One dial, one button, the whole pond

The box at the water's edge holds the inverter and the screen. The layout never moves: status on top, the one thing that matters on the left, the platform map on the right, actions below. Turn the dial to move between moments, or press Locate.

03 · Installation

Built on the bank, paired by touch

The modules clip on in any order, so the unit does not know where each one sits. Pairing fixes that: press a bracket lip, it lights white, and its place fills in on the screen. Try it in step 4 by tapping the modules below.

The platform

04 · On the deck

One trip out, in four moments

When someone has to go out by boat, a rugged tablet goes with them. The water, the shore unit and the tablet follow the same task, step by step. Tap the tablet's main button to move on, or play the whole trip.
